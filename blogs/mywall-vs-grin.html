What does a Shopify or DTC brand need in its first 30 days?

A first creator campaign needs five milestones, and only the first is mostly about software.

  • Platform set up: account created, brand profile added, team invited.
  • Creators shortlisted: relevant creators found and checked for audience fit and content quality.
  • Creators onboarded: outreach sent, terms agreed, briefs and usage rights confirmed.
  • Campaign live: product shipped, content approved and posted.
  • Early results measured: reach, engagement, clicks and sales reviewed.

Software only partly controls launch speed. Product stock, creator response rates, shipping times, approval rounds, budget and your team's hours matter just as much. A platform that saves 10 minutes at setup but adds days to shortlisting won't get you live sooner.

Pricing, onboarding and hidden time costs

Judge each platform by what it costs to get your first campaign live, not by the subscription price alone.

GRIN publishes its pricing. Plans run from Free ($0) to Starter ($200/month), Growth ($500/month) and Scale ($1,500/month), with Enterprise priced separately. On top of the plan, GRIN charges a management fee on wallet deposits used to pay creators: 20% on Free, 10% on Starter, 7% on Growth, and 5% on Scale. Gifting-only campaigns need no deposit, and self-serve plans are month-to-month.

MyWall doesn't publish pricing, so request a quote. A 3-day free trial, demos, and fully managed campaigns are available.

Also factor in:

  • Spend-based fees: GRIN's deposit fee grows with creator payments.
  • Integrations: confirm Shopify, email, and payments on your exact plan.
  • Operating time: weekly hours on outreach, follow-ups, and reporting, where automation pays off.
